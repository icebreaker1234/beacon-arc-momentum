# Beacon Arc Launch

Build a complete, polished website for **Beacon Arc**, a technology company that helps small and medium-sized businesses improve how they work through custom software, websites, automation, integrations, and practical AI solutions.

Use these sites only as references for quality, pacing, and the feeling of a carefully choreographed web experience:
- https://abatable.com/
- https://www.haroonrashidi.com/

Do **not** copy their branding, layouts, text, images, or animations. Create an original visual identity and motion language that clearly belongs to Beacon Arc.

## Brand and message

Beacon Arc is a dependable, thoughtful technology partner. We help businesses replace repetitive tasks, disconnected tools, and unclear processes with systems that make work easier to manage.

Use this as the central brand idea:

**Better systems. Clearer momentum.**

Support it with plain, customer-focused language such as:

**We design and build software, automation, and AI solutions that help growing businesses spend less time fighting their tools and more time moving forward.**

Make the value clear to non-technical business owners. Explain what we do, what business problems we solve, and what it’s like to work with us. Avoid vague tech jargon, exaggerated promises, and generic claims about “revolutionizing” businesses.

## Original art direction

Create a distinctive Beacon Arc identity around clarity, connection, and forward motion:

- Design a simple wordmark and geometric symbol using a focused point of light and a precise curved arc. Do not use a literal lighthouse illustration.
- Use a premium palette: deep ink or midnight, warm off-white, and one restrained luminous accent such as cyan, mint, or amber.
- Combine confident typography, generous spacing, precise grids, fine lines, and subtle gradients.
- Make custom visuals from CSS, SVG, and polished software interface mockups. Avoid generic stock imagery, circuit-board patterns, AI brains, and random AI-generated graphics.
- The site should feel quietly futuristic and trustworthy: visually memorable, but never flashy or difficult to read.

## Signature scroll experience

Create one bespoke visual story that expresses Beacon Arc’s work. It should feel like a purposeful, high-quality experience—not a collection of standard fade-in effects.

As the visitor scrolls through the hero, a focused point of light draws a precise arc. That arc gradually becomes connected nodes in a business workflow, then resolves into a polished software interface. Continue the visual idea subtly through the page so the arc can connect a business problem to a practical solution and outcome.

Use refined scroll choreography, visual transitions, layered depth, and carefully timed reveals to guide attention. The experience should have the polish and pacing of the reference sites, but the animation itself must express Beacon Arc’s own theme.

Keep ordinary scrolling natural. Never trap, hijack, or force-scroll the visitor. Keep the headline, body copy, and calls to action readable and available throughout the animation. Make the page useful even if animations are disabled.

## Optional cursor-reactive sound

Add a subtle, original ambient soundscape to the hero:

- Sound is **off by default**. Never autoplay.
- Add a small, clearly labeled **Sound off / Sound on** control that is keyboard-accessible and works with screen readers.
- Start audio only after the visitor explicitly turns it on.
- When enabled, let the cursor’s position gently modulate the ambient tone while it moves across the hero. Use smooth, low-volume changes; do not play a separate loud sound for every pointer movement.
- Include a quick mute control and remember the visitor’s choice in the current browser.
- Keep the website fully usable with sound off. Respect reduced-motion and audio preferences. Disable or simplify the sound interaction on touch devices.
- Use a lightweight Web Audio implementation. Do not add large audio files or tracking.

## Homepage structure

Build the homepage as a clear story for a business owner:

1. **Hero:**  
   Eyebrow: “Software, automation & AI for growing businesses”  
   Headline: “Better systems. Clearer momentum.”  
   Short supporting copy explaining that Beacon Arc turns time-consuming processes and disconnected tools into useful software, automation, and AI workflows.  
   Primary CTA: **Book a discovery call**  
   Secondary CTA: **Explore what we build**  
   Show the signature arc-to-workflow animation without obscuring the message.

2. **The friction:**  
   Show familiar business problems: repetitive work, disconnected systems, manual reporting, slow handoffs, and unclear data. Use concise examples, not fear-based copy.

3. **The shift:**  
   Show a visual “before and after” where scattered tasks and tools become a clear, connected workflow. Explain the practical business benefit.

4. **Services:**  
   Introduce the services below with clear links to the Services page.

5. **Selected work:**  
   Show detailed case-study cards that explain the challenge, solution, and verified outcomes.

6. **How we work:**  
   Explain the process: **Discover → Design → Build → Improve**. Set expectations about communication, scope, feedback, launch, and ongoing support.

7. **Trust:**  
   Explain Beacon Arc’s practical approach: transparent recommendations, maintainable systems, security-minded implementation, documentation, and clear ownership. Do not invent client logos, statistics, awards, testimonials, or certifications.

8. **Closing CTA:**  
   Invite the visitor to describe one process they want to improve. Use **Let’s talk** or **Book a discovery call**.

## Services

Create a dedicated Services page and clear service sections for:

- **Custom business software:** internal tools, management systems, customer portals, and purpose-built applications.
- **Websites and digital products:** professional websites, customer experiences, and digital portals.
- **Workflow automation:** connect existing tools, reduce repetitive tasks, and improve handoffs.
- **AI solutions:** assistants, document and data workflows, and AI features integrated into real business processes.
- **Dashboards and data systems:** make operational information easier to understand and act on.
- **Integrations and cloud systems:** connect platforms and build secure, maintainable foundations.

For each service explain who it helps, the common problem it addresses, what Beacon Arc can deliver, what a sensible first project could look like, and how to get started. Do not promise guaranteed savings or claim AI can replace all staff.

## Work and case studies

Create a premium work listing and case-study detail layout. Each case study should cover:

- Business context
- Challenge
- Solution
- Relevant product or workflow visuals
- Verified result
- Technologies used, when relevant

Include a case study concept for a **co-living or hostel operations platform**, covering resident profiles, room occupancy, rent tracking, expenses, and publishing room availability to a website. Do not invent a client name, claim a live deployment, or make up business results. If it is not a verified live project, label it honestly as a **pilot**, **prototype**, or **product concept**.

Never fabricate customers, testimonials, logos, awards, revenue growth, time saved, user numbers, or performance metrics. Make any missing proof easy to add later.

## About and contact

Create an About page that explains Beacon Arc’s working principles without inventing company history, employee biographies, credentials, or office details.

Create a Contact page with a usable form for name, work email, company, what they want to improve, optional budget range, and preferred contact method. Include validation and clear success and error states. Do not show a fake successful submission if no form service is connected; make the real integration straightforward and clearly identify any required setup.

## Navigation and footer

Use a clean sticky header with **Services**, **Work**, **About**, and a strong **Let’s talk** CTA. Include **Insights** only if a real insights page is built.

Create a complete footer with useful navigation, contact details as editable placeholders, Privacy Policy, Terms, and a copyright year that updates automatically. Do not invent an email, phone number, or address.

## Implementation and quality

- Inspect the existing Lovable project first and keep its current framework and working integrations where possible.
- Build the pages and interactions directly; do not only return a plan or a static mockup.
- Make the website responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Use semantic markup, accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, meaningful alt text, and visible focus states.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; offer a calm version of the experience.
- Optimize fonts, images, animation, and loading performance.
- Add page titles, meta descriptions, Open Graph metadata, and a clean heading structure.
- Ensure navigation, buttons, and form states work. Do not leave decorative controls pretending to be functional.
- Use reusable components and consistent design tokens.
- Review the finished preview from the hero through the footer on desktop and mobile. Refine the signature animation until it feels original to Beacon Arc, communicates our work clearly, and supports trust rather than distracting from it.
